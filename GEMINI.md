# Auto Inspect — South African vehicle checks

- `vehicle_vin_check` (VIN format, country, manufacturer, check digit), `sa_licence_disc_status` (expiry and late-renewal penalty estimate; provinces differ), `sa_number_plate_check` (which province a plate format points to), `sa_find_test_stations` (roadworthy testing stations from the official registers) and `autoinspect_verify_report` (is an Auto Inspect report genuine).
- These are deterministic checks, not a lookup of the owner, finance or police status of a vehicle. Quote each result's own caveats.
